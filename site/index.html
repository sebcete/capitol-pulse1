TRANSPARENCY

The fine print.
Front and center.

What does “top 10” mean?

Politicians are ranked by equal-weight average percentage return from their publicly reported stock purchases in your chosen filing window. Only trades with a verifiable post-disclosure entry close and a later current close qualify; at least two qualifying purchases are needed. Each reported buy counts once, independent of disclosed amount.

Hypothetical return calculation

Investing starts at the first available market close strictly after the filing date, not when the politician traded. Return = (latest available closing price ÷ hypothetical entry closing price − 1) × 100. Dollar simulation applies that return to your chosen amount, independently per selected ticker.

Are those real portfolios?

No. Transaction disclosures show buy/sell events and broad dollar brackets. They usually do not show share counts, precise executions, current share ownership or a complete brokerage portfolio. We display reported activity and hypothetical performance, not confirmed holdings or profit.

What do the growth labels mean?

Momentum and volatility labels are mechanical descriptions of recent historical prices—not an AI forecast or a buy recommendation. “Explosive” means high recent volatility; it may lose value rapidly. “Steady” means more modest recent movement, not guaranteed safety.

When does the app update?

The website checks a stored snapshot whenever opened, and GitHub Actions attempts to fetch new disclosures and price history daily. The latest source filing date and the latest price-bar date appear in the interface. Scheduled jobs, public data sources, and free market feeds can fail or lag.

Data and sources

Congressional transaction records: Austin Starks public House and Senate dataset, derived from House filings and Senate disclosures. Prices: Yahoo Finance historical daily prices (when available). Source filing links appear with individual trades. Dataset coverage and dates are visible in the app; this is not a complete holdings database.

Not investment advice

This free personal research tool is not affiliated with Congress, Autopilot, or any broker. Public filings can be corrected or delayed; prices can be stale or inaccurate; missing data is not estimated. Always research a company's business, valuation, earnings, and risk before investing.
